# Charging dashboard background

Build 21 retains the [illuminated energy core](energy-core-v1.md) and [original reference logo and artwork](original-artwork-v1.md), and adds compact controls and an optional [Android screen saver](../../docs/charging-screen-saver.md). The [Build 18 live underwater version](underwater-v1.md) is saved with its APK and source in [the rollback archive](saved/build-18-underwater/README.md). The aurora below and the [Build 17 instrument-bay design](cyberpunk-v1.md) are earlier alternatives.

Asset: `assets/charging/teal_aurora.webp` (1024 × 1536, 39,710 bytes).

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool, then encoded as WebP at quality 86. No proprietary app designs or assets were used. The original generated PNG is retained by the image tool; the project consumes the bundled WebP. This is static decoration: Flutter renders the actual percentage, live gauge, measurements, and controls above it. A dark overlay protects text contrast. The decoded background width is capped at 720 pixels, and background and gauge rendering are isolated in repaint boundaries.

## Final generation prompt

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production mobile app background image only, portrait 2:3 composition (1024 x 1536 if possible), for Fittery's premium charging dashboard. Create an elegant, very dark abstract aurora with sculptural translucent flowing glass/silk ribbons and beautifully smooth color falloff. Palette: near-black midnight blue and deep petrol teal dominate, luminous jade teal and cool aqua at the edges with a very small blue-violet accent. Compose a restrained luminous curved ribbon near the upper right edge and another near the lower left edge; keep the entire central 65 percent wide vertical column calm, dark, softly graded negative space for legible white interface text, a live circular gauge in the upper half, and measurement cards in the lower half. The atmosphere is refined and premium, soft dimensional lighting and flowing optical glass, no busy stars, particles, grids or noise. Most pixels should be very dark. This is wallpaper artwork, NOT a UI mockup: absolutely no text, letters, numbers, logos, batteries, icons, circles, progress indicators, widgets, cards, device frames or watermark. No harsh bright white areas or blown out neon. The background will remain static and the app will render all actual text and animations above it.
