# Fittery store listing draft

## App name

Fittery

## Short description

Battery insights, local history and charging alerts. Private and ad-free.

## Full description

Understand your phone's battery with clear live readings, local history, and alerts you control.

Fittery shows battery percentage, voltage, net current, battery-side power, temperature, and the hardware information Android makes available. Explore recent readings in interactive graphs and review observed charging and discharging sessions.

Set charging-target, low-battery, and temperature alerts with your preferred alarm tone. Optional background monitoring keeps recording while you use other apps, with a visible notification and a stop control.

• Live battery readings and interactive telemetry graphs
• Charging and discharging history stored on your phone
• Configurable charging, low-battery, and temperature alerts
• Optional live device-wide network activity on Dashboard
• System, dark, and AMOLED themes, plus temperature and voltage units
• No ads, accounts, analytics, or tracking SDKs

Your readings stay on your device. Monitor recordings save charging and discharging measurements locally. Completed recordings expire 30 days after creation by default; you can keep them until manual deletion, browse by date, and clear recordings in Settings.

Some readings, including cycle count and capacity, depend on your device and may be unavailable. Battery-health percentages and time remaining are estimates. Power is measured at the battery and can differ from charger wattage. Charging-target alerts remind you to unplug; they cannot stop charging. Android and manufacturer background restrictions can interrupt monitoring.
