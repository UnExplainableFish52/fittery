## Included changes

- Optional charging dashboard, disabled by default, with the centered Fittery/fish mark, teal glass-wave backdrop and inner artwork, live percentage arc, restrained pulse/particles, and four statistic cards.
- Five-second, time-weighted rough charging estimates in minutes to the enabled charge target or full. No extra sensor polling; unavailable or unsuitable measurements remain unavailable.
- Consistent AM/PM clock below the ring, truthful connection/tracking time, monotonic elapsed minutes, slide dismissal, and installed-version footer.
- Optional Android DreamService screen-saver integration, controlled through Fittery and Android settings. System/OEM idle behavior applies; device authentication is preserved.
- Original dedicated charge-limit, temperature, and low-battery voice recordings; independent custom/system/Silent choices with previews and persistent local-file access.
- Battery-tap dashboard entry, compact monitor/dashboard controls, and smooth settings switches.
- New FAQ: **How can I support the developer?** It invites a personal message through the developer website or direct email and explains how much the encouragement means to the developer. No new permission or dependency was added for this FAQ.
