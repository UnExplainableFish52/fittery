# Fittery v1.2.0 (Build 27)

**Know Your Battery.**

Welcome to Fittery's first GitHub release! Fittery is a lightweight Android battery monitor built with Flutter, Dart, and native Kotlin integrations. It brings together live battery insights, local recordings, charging dashboards, and configurable alerts, without accounts, ads, analytics, or a remote service.

This is the first release published on GitHub, rather than the first version of the app. It includes Fittery's existing capabilities alongside the quality and feature improvements introduced in v1.2.0.

## What's new in v1.2.0

### A stronger Fittery identity

- New blue-to-mint Fittery app icon and branded splash screen.
- Updated Android launcher sizes and a transparent adaptive-icon foreground.
- A compact Settings header featuring the logo, application name, and **Know Your Battery** tagline.
- Consistent branding across the dashboard, onboarding, and prepared store icon.

### Independent Background Alerts

Background Alerts is a separate, optional way to watch battery percentage and temperature while the app's UI is closed. It uses Android battery broadcasts and your existing alert thresholds and sounds, without enabling Monitor Mode or creating recordings.

- A separate Settings switch controls Background Alerts.
- Alert-only operation avoids continuous statistics polling, hardware discovery, and recording.
- A quiet, low-importance foreground-service notification makes the active feature visible.
- When Background Alerts and Monitor Mode are both enabled, they share one service notification and one threshold evaluator to avoid duplicate alarms.
- Stopping either feature leaves the other enabled feature running.
- Supported boot and app-update recovery reuse the user's saved choices.
- Dismissing the status notification, where Android permits it, does not disable Background Alerts. Use the switch or explicit Stop action to turn it off.

**Monitor Mode retains its existing live statistics, recordings, graphs, and alarms.** Background Alerts adds an independent way to receive configured alarms without recording.

This is an experimental reliability improvement. Android battery-event delivery, power management, and manufacturer restrictions determine actual timing and availability; alerts do not have a guaranteed delivery deadline.

### Easier setup and feature discovery

- A one-time Settings introduction highlights dashboards, lock-screen options, alarms, sounds, monitoring, refresh intervals, graphs, and history.
- After acknowledging the introduction, users can choose **Enable Background Alerts** or **Not Now**. The feature remains available in Settings.
- The manual Charging Dashboard is enabled by default for new installations.
- Existing installations retain their previous Charging Dashboard preference.
- The optional charging screen saver remains off until selected by the user, with a visible Android setup shortcut.

### Cleaner dialogs and clearer help

- App-owned decision dialogs use full-width, vertically stacked actions for more readable long labels.
- The recommended action uses a filled button, with an outlined alternative beneath it. Destructive confirmations emphasize keeping data.
- FAQ topics now start with the app introduction, tech stack, features, and setup, followed by dashboards, Background Alerts versus Monitor Mode, graphs, and history.
- Existing battery-use guidance and troubleshooting are preserved; bug reporting and developer support appear toward the bottom.

## Included in this first GitHub release

### Battery insights

View battery percentage, charging status, temperature, voltage, net current, battery-side power, and supported hardware information. Explore live telemetry and available estimates for charging time and remaining battery time. Measurements and hardware details vary by device.

Fittery reports battery-side measurements. These are not the charger's rated power, and estimates are not independent measurements of battery degradation.

### Charging Dashboard and screen saver

Open the full-screen charging dashboard while plugged in using the main battery display, the **Fullscreen Dashboard** button, or Settings. It includes the cosmic dashboard presentation and live charging information. Unplugging closes the dashboard.

The optional **Charging dashboard on lock screen** feature uses Android's screen-saver system. Select Fittery in Android settings to complete setup. Android and the device manufacturer determine idle activation and lock-screen behavior; the feature does not bypass device authentication or promise immediate activation when connecting a charger.

### Configurable alarms

Configure charging-target, low-battery, and temperature thresholds. Choose supported system tones or an audio file, preview sounds, and silence or snooze active alarms. Audible alarms use the phone's alarm volume.

Fittery reminds you when a threshold is reached. It cannot stop charging automatically.

### Recordings, graphs, and local history

Monitor Mode records battery activity for later review. Browse recordings by date, inspect measurement graphs and charging summaries, rename recordings, and delete recordings you no longer need.

Completed recordings expire after 30 days by default. Enable **Keep recordings until I delete them** to retain them; active recordings are protected. Unsupported measurements remain unavailable, and gaps can represent periods with no recorded readings.

### Personalization and privacy

Adjust display preferences, units, alert sounds, notification contents, and notification refresh intervals. The 1-, 3-, or 5-second notification refresh setting controls the live Monitor Mode notification; it does not change the three-second recording interval or enable polling in alert-only mode.

Readings, recordings, preferences, and audio references stay on the device. The release app does not request Internet permission. Optional website and email interactions use external applications.

## Download and installation

- **`Fittery-1.2.0-build27.apk`** — install this on a compatible Android phone. Android may ask you to allow installation from the application opening the APK.
- **`Fittery-1.2.0-build27.aab`** — Android App Bundle for Play Console distribution; it is not directly installable on a phone.
- GitHub's automatically generated source archives contain source code, not an installed application.

An in-place APK update preserves app data when package identity and signing certificates match. A GitHub APK signed with the upload key may not replace a Google Play installation signed with a different Play App Signing certificate. Do not uninstall to resolve a signing conflict without first considering your local data: uninstalling removes Fittery's private settings and recordings.

To try Background Alerts, open Settings, enable the feature, allow notifications when prompted, and configure your thresholds and sounds. To record statistics, enable Monitor Mode separately.

## Known Android limitations

- Force-stop and Android's **Active apps → Stop** action can stop the app and its background work. They differ from dismissing a notification or swiping away a task.
- Foreground-notification dismissal depends on Android version and device state. It may remain unavailable while the phone is locked or through Clear all.
- Manufacturer battery optimization or autostart restrictions may interrupt monitoring or delay alerts.
- Battery measurements, cycle counts, health information, and estimates depend on what the device exposes.
- Lock-screen screen-saver activation is controlled by Android and the manufacturer.
- The foreground-service declaration and demonstration video remain necessary for Play submission. GitHub publication does not imply Google Play approval.
