Fittery Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Fittery is developed by TheIdealDev52 (Saksham Sharma). Its Android package is com.fittery.tid52. For privacy questions or support, contact sharma@saksham.info.np.

Information processed on your device

Fittery reads battery measurements and basic device information to display battery status, estimate health and remaining time, record local history, and deliver battery and temperature alerts. Available readings depend on your phone's hardware and Android firmware.

Optional dashboard network activity reads Android's device-wide traffic counters and current connection information only while Dashboard is visible. Wi-Fi names are displayed only when Android makes them available without extra sensitive permissions. Network readings and names are not saved or uploaded. Fittery does not perform internet speed tests. Device specifications are read on demand.

Collection and sharing

Battery samples, session records, preferences, and the selected alarm-tone reference are stored on your device. Fittery does not upload this information, create accounts, run analytics, display advertisements, or include tracking SDKs. The release app does not request Internet permission. Android cloud backup is disabled for the app.

Permissions and background monitoring

Notifications deliver alerts and show the optional foreground monitoring service. You choose whether to enable monitoring, which alert thresholds and sounds to use, and whether notifications appear on the lock screen. Monitoring does not start automatically at boot. You can stop it in the app or through the monitoring notification.

Android's normal network-state and Wi-Fi-state permissions support the optional connection display. Fittery does not request location permission or nearby-device permission to reveal restricted Wi-Fi names. The system alarm picker provides your selected tone; Fittery does not upload audio. The battery optimization control opens Android settings so you can review background restrictions.

Retention and deletion

Monitor mode saves three-second battery measurements for both charging and discharging in the app's private database. Each monitor recording keeps its original data; graphs are generated when viewed. Completed recordings expire 30 days after creation by default, with no count limit. Active recordings are protected. The "Keep recordings until I delete them" setting disables automatic deletion. Cleanup runs when recording or viewing history, so deletion can occur later if the app is not running. "Clear all recordings" removes all local recordings and the legacy telemetry cache. Uninstalling the app removes its private data. The legacy dashboard telemetry cache is separate and retains its existing short-term limits.

External links and support

Privacy-policy and developer-website links open your browser. The browser and destination website operate under their own privacy policies. If you email the developer, the information you choose to send is handled through email to respond to your inquiry. Avoid sending information you do not want to share. Deleting local app history does not delete emails you have sent; contact the address above about an existing support message.

Measurement limitations

Battery health percentages, capacity estimates, power, and time remaining depend on device readings and are labeled estimates where applicable. They are not independent measurements of battery degradation. Charging-limit alerts remind you to unplug and cannot stop charging.
